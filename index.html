This day's sheet: front line, boundaries and divisions as drawn by 12th Army Group. Earlier sheets, one colour per day (see the key above) Eighth Air Force: heavy bombers from England to strategic and tactical targets Ninth Air Force: medium bombers and fighter-bombers in close support Area covered by a sheet in this set

Maps. HQ 12th Army Group (HQ FUSAG until July 1944) G-3 situation maps, Library of Congress Geography & Map Division (G5701.S7 svar .A4).

Ground. Condensed from Mary H. Williams, Chronology 1941–1945, US Army in World War II, Center of Military History (HyperWar edition); a few quiet days filled in from standard histories.

Air. Kit C. Carter & Robert Mueller, The Army Air Forces in World War II: Combat Chronology 1941–1945, via the McKillop USAAF chronology (Aircrew Remembered). Aircraft counts are bombers attacking each target. Targets are placed at town level from GeoNames.

Routes are schematic: each line runs from the force's base area to the target. The chronology does not record the actual courses, assembly points or turning points.
